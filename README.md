# It's Up


<p align="center"><img src="ItsUp/icon.png"></p>

<p align="center"><i>Yes. Still up. Press it.</i></p>


Doing your rotation is hard enough without having to stare at your hotbars. This plugin helps you know exactly when an ability you actually care about is back up.

We don't want to clutter your screen with a massive status board. This won't show you everything that's currently available—instead, **it stays completely empty until you use an ability in combat.** Once you spend it and it comes off cooldown, the icon pops up to remind you. Press it, and it's gone again. 

Because it only reminds you about things you've *already* used, it won't spam your screen before a pull. And for abilities with charges, it only pings when you go from 0 to 1—if you're sitting on a charge, it stays out of your way since you can already press it. 

It's perfect for those key rotational abilities you really don't want to drift. You can also configure each ability so the reminder stays on screen until you press it, or quietly disappears after a few seconds.

## How to use it

<p align="center"><img src="docs/move-mode.png" alt="The panel showing several tracked icons, with the gold anchor marker"><br><i>Position the panel</i></p>

<p align="center"><img src="docs/warming-icon.gif" alt="Cooldown animation showing in a action"><br><i>A icon counts down just before an ability returns</i></p>

- `/itsup` — pick which abilities to track
- `/itsup move` — unlock the panel to drag it into place

Everything else — filters, timing meanings, panel positioning — is explained in the settings window itself.
